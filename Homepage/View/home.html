How to Prevent Dengue

  1. Remove stagnant water from containers.
  2. Keep water-storage containers covered.
  3. Use mosquito nets and suitable repellents.
  4. Wear protective clothing when possible.
  5. Keep your surroundings clean.

Important Health Notice

This website provides awareness and educational information. The risk checker is not a medical diagnosis. Seek professional medical care for serious warning signs.
